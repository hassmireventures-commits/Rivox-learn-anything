Frequently asked questions

Do I need my own AI API key?

No — Rivox includes Built-in AI with a free daily quota, so you can start generating quizzes immediately. If you want more, you can bring your own key for OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, or any OpenAI-compatible provider.

Is my data private?

Rivox is local-first — your quiz history, learning paths, progress, and API keys stay on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers. Content only reaches an AI provider when you generate something, and only to the provider you chose.

Can I practice job interviews with AI?

Yes. Rivox's voice interview mode runs HR and technical interview drills — you speak your answer out loud, Rivox transcribes it with live captions, and an AI rubric scores your response.

Does Rivox help with competitive exams like SSC or banking?

Yes — the Career and Exam modes include syllabus-aware mock exams and competitive-exam-style question practice, alongside a skill matrix and interview drills for job prep.

Is Rivox free?

Yes. Rivox is ad-supported with a free Built-in AI quota (watch a rewarded ad for more). There's no required account and no subscription — bring your own API key if you want unlimited generation on your own usage.
