Get it on Google Play What is an agentic tutor?

A tutor, not just a chatbot

The chat knows your goal, your plan, and your recent quizzes. Ask it to quiz you and the questions appear right in the conversation, with an explanation for every miss.

It fixes its own plan

Got a quiz that missed the point, or a module you do not need? Say so. Rivox renames, adds, drops, or reorders modules and rebuilds the quiz, with Undo if you change your mind.

Any skill, taught the right way

Exam-style papers for IELTS, NEET, or JEE. Code-reading checks for programming. Form cues and safety for workouts. Tool and safety steps for DIY. Technique checks for art and music.

A streak that forgives one bad day

Every quiz and quick question counts toward your streak. A seven-day run earns a streak freeze that covers one missed day. XP and levels show how far you have come.

Answer from the notification

Once a day Rivox asks one question from your recent mistakes. Tap A, B, or C on the notification and see if you were right, without opening the app. Turn it off any time.

Built-in AI, or your own key

You can start generating without setup. If you want more quota or a specific model, add an OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, or other compatible key in Settings.

Quizzes and paths that follow you

Tell Rivox what you are studying. It writes quizzes and module paths around your goals and weak topics. Upload notes or a resume if you want the questions grounded in your own material.

Voice interview practice

Answer HR and technical drills out loud. You get live captions while you speak, then a rubric score on the open questions.

A small pack each day

Each day you get an article or video pick, a quiz, and optional reminders. You can change the alarm sound if you want.

Flashcards from your misses

Wrong answers and uploaded notes can become cards. They come back on a spaced schedule, close to when you would forget them.

Career and exam prep

Use a skill matrix, interview drills, syllabus-aware mocks, and competitive-style reasoning practice for exams such as SSC, banking, and CAT-style papers.

Progress stays local

Quiz history, path completion, and your library live on the device. You control them. Sign-in is optional if you later want backup.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI tutor app?

An AI tutor app teaches you rather than just answering. Rivox keeps your goal and your plan, decides the next session, quizzes you, explains your mistakes, and changes the plan when it gets something wrong. Read more in What is an agentic AI tutor?

Can I learn something that is not school or an exam?

Yes. Rivox adapts to fitness, cooking, DIY, drawing, music, game development, coding, a new job, or anything else you name. It changes how it quizzes you to suit the skill.

Do I need my own AI API key?

No. Built-in AI is ready when you open the app. You get a free daily quota. If you want more, you can add your own key for OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, or another OpenAI-compatible provider.

Is my data private?

Your quizzes, paths, progress, and keys stay on your device. We do not host an account database for that data. Content only goes to an AI provider when you generate something, and only to the provider you chose.

Can I practice job interviews with AI?

Yes. You can run HR and technical drills, speak your answer out loud, see live captions, and get a rubric score on the response.

Does Rivox help with competitive exams like SSC or banking?

Yes. Career and Exam modes include syllabus-aware mocks, competitive-style practice, a skill matrix, and interview drills.

Is Rivox free?

Yes. Rivox is ad-supported. You get a free Built-in AI quota, and you can watch a rewarded ad for more. Sign-in is optional. There is no subscription. Add your own API key if you want unlimited generation on your own usage.
