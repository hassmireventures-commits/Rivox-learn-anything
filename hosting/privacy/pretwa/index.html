Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Pretwa: Ancient Wheel Checkers ("the App") is published by Hassmire Ventures. This policy explains what the App handles when you use it.

1. Our approach

Most of your play stays on the device. We use a few Google services so the App can show ads, record crashes, tune feature flags, and host optional online matches. We do not create an email-and-password account for you.

2. Information stored on your device

The following stays on your device unless you uninstall the App or clear its data:

  • Display name and settings (sound, animation speed, last board, welcome and daily-challenge prompts)
  • Local match stats, puzzle progress, daily-challenge streak, and the daily play counter
  • A saved in-progress match, if you leave one
  • Online room codes you have created or joined

3. Accounts and online play

The App signs you in anonymously with Firebase Authentication so a host and a guest can share a room. Firestore stores room codes, board state, move logs, and those anonymous participant ids. There is no email or password. Reinstalling the App creates a new anonymous id; rooms on the old id cannot be recovered.

4. Analytics, crash reports, and remote config

Firebase Analytics records events such as starting a match, finishing a tutorial lesson, or reaching the daily play limit. Firebase Crashlytics records crash stacks so we can fix bugs. Firebase Remote Config fetches feature flags (ads, play limits, hint paywall) from Google's servers.

5. Advertising

When ads are enabled, Google AdMob may show banner ads (Home and match results), native ads (Settings and waiting room, Android), and rewarded ads (extra daily plays and optional extra hints). AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and usage data under Google's ads policies. In regions that require it, we ask for consent first (Google UMP).

Our website (learn-anything-43970.web.app) uses Google AdSense to show ads. See Google's advertising policies.

6. Notifications

After the tutorial, the App may ask for permission to send a local reminder when a daily-challenge streak is at risk. That reminder is scheduled on the device. We do not currently send push notifications for online turns.

7. What we do not collect

We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, government IDs, payment cards, precise location, contacts, or photos through the App's core features.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data retention and deletion

Local data remains until you clear it or uninstall the App. Online rooms expire according to the service. You can email hassmireventures@gmail.com with privacy questions.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the in-app document and this page's "Last updated" date.
