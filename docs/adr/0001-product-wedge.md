# ADR 0001 — Product wedge

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-13  
**Owners:** Product / CEO

## Context

Learn Anything ships learning, exam prep, career prep, PKB/RAG, local LLM, reminders, and multi-provider BYOK in one client. ARB reviews found activation blocked by API-key setup and unclear category leadership.

## Decision

**Primary wedge (next 90 days):** *Daily adaptive quiz + guided study for a single learner goal* (learning mode first; exam countdown as the strongest secondary).

- First-run value: **demo quiz without an API key** (already wired in onboarding).
- Cloud/local AI setup is **optional unlock**, not a gate.
- Career matrix, deep PKB, and marketplace stay **secondary** until weekly retention and NPS justify depth.

## Consequences

- Roadmap prioritizes activation, quiz quality, and trust gates over new modes.
- Incomplete exam/career metrics should be hidden or honesty-bannered until real.
- Freemium hosted AI remains a 90-day design item, not a blocker for demo-first UX.
